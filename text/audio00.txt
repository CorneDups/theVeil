Prologue

The following story is a personal account and reflection on some of the happiest, darkest, most mysterious, and most meaningful events of my life so far. I have done my best to remain as true as possible to my memories, yet I am human and have most likely erred, especially in recalling my darkest moments. If so, please know that it is not intentional, and I humbly ask for your kindness in pardoning me.

All character names—except for Hicks, Jana, Yollande, Gerrie de Haas, Henk, and Hendrik—have been changed to protect their identities. This short book is not intended to intrude upon the stories of others or to tear anyone down, as our battle is not against flesh and blood. Yet every name represents a real person with their own remarkable story.

As you step into my life, I hope you see both my brokenness and my humanity—but more than that, I pray you witness Christ’s redemptive power in the midst of my deepest sorrows. I did join a cult and became a devoted follower, willing to sacrifice everything for its message. Yet God promises that if we truly seek, we will find—and what I found was far more than I could have ever imagined. This portion of the book seeks to convey that mystery.

Finally, my prayer for you, the reader, is that in the quietness of your own soul, you might pause, reflect, and ask yourself: how has Jesus been uniquely pursuing your heart?
